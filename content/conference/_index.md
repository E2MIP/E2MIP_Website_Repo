The E2MIP challenge will conclude with a short workshop held alongside the 2024 MICCAI conference. During this event the results from the challenge will be presented together with a selection of well-performing and scientifically outstanding approaches. 

We are currently in the process of establishing the detailed program, so the information might be changing. 

**🕑 Date:** 6th of October 2024

**🕑 Duration:** 2h

**🌍 Location:** Palmeraie Conference Centre, MARRAKESH / MOROCCO

**📰 Program: (tentative)** 

- Welcome Message
- Welcome Message from our sponsors
- 1st participants presentation
- 2nd participants presentation
- 3th participants presentation
- Presentation of the results
- Final remarks
